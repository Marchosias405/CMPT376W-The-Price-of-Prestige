## Colour cues

Green choice boxes indicate support or protection, amber indicates uncertain tradeoffs, and red indicates heightened risk or continuing harm. Labels explain each hue. Endings use matching green, teal, amber, or red backgrounds while preserving their stated costs. These are contextual cues, not guaranteed results.
