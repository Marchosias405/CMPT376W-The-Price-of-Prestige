Scenario Description

The player is a student who just got a full-time job offer on a research team working on Augmented Reality (AR) technologies. The player’s supervisor, Max, is a big name in AR research. After working with Max for some time, the player realizes that Max constantly loses temper over small mistakes and uses abusive language to his employee. The player needs to decide what to do at different events happening in the story. The player can talk to management, or keep their head down and keep working.

**Characters:**
Diane: Main character

Max: Research team lead, Team supervisor.
Is prone to anger over minor mistakes, and expresses said anger by berating and yelling at employees either over text or in-person.

Jean: Management that oversees this research team.
Is complicit in Max’s behaviours and doesn’t stop them and tells people to deal with max’s behaviour.

**Ethical Dilemmas:**
How will your actions impact the team, when you find the bug you are given the decision of getting the person who introduced said bug at the end of Max’s ire or fix it yourself despite not being the right person to do so.
The second major ethical dilemma a player can run into is whether to sue or not, suing could lead to the end of the team and therefore cause the rest of the team to lose their jobs but not suing will mean max can continue to abuse the other team members.

**Lesson Goals:**
To teach about how you handle situations where you will experience harm if you don’t cause harm to others, and how you handle causing harm to others.
