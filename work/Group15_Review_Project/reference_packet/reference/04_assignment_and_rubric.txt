G1 - ACM Code of Ethics Case Study (Twine)

develop an ethical case study scenario based on one of the ACM Code of Ethics Case Studies. Similar to the Rogue Services CYOA online activity, your scenario will be realized as a choose-your-own-adventure (CYOA) game using Twine (https://twinery.org).

Submisiion:

Twine project file. The Twine HTML file or a .zip file containing your Twine HTML file and any necessary assets. If you used images, audio, or video files, they must be included in your .zip file or externally hosted; you must ensure (by testing) that they work when published.
Design Document. A PDF document that describes the design of your project according to the required design sections outlined below.

Design Document:
An important part of this project is the creation and usage of a design process and document. Before you begin working in Twine, you will create an initial design that identifies and briefly describes the main characters, the scenario, and the different choices. Each team member must contribute different perspective-elements to the story and your team must identify who contributed what. Your design document will have the following sections:

Scenario Description: A short description of the scenario.
Characters: List and describe each character in the story.
Ethical Dilemmas: List and describe the ethical dilemmas the characters face and the choices that are made available to them and a rationale for each choice (that is consistent to those characters).
Lesson Goals: List and describe the lesson goals that your story is intended to provide.
Story Flow: Provide a sketch of the proposed and final branching flow of your story.
Team Contributions: In a table, list each of the sections above and list which team members contributed and their percentage of contribution to that section.

Grading:
Clearly identify which case study you are representing and provide a link to its ACM page.
Clearly identify the role of each group member.
Ensure that the scenario is complete and playable without additional instructions.
Ensure that your scenario extends or modifies the original case study text in some way, i.e., don't just copy the text from the source.
Ensure that the choices presented are realistic and coherent with the case study.
Ensure that more than one perspective is represented, i.e., that the story is branching based on different ideas or choices.
Presents a visually interesting, consistent and coherent interface.
Finally, ensure that the scenario is coherent with the case under study, i.e., does the scenario capture the essence or ideas of the case under study?

Grading Rubric

Twine Story (/25)

Intro Screen (0 pts, -2 if any item is missing)
Story Name.
ACM Case Study Link. Clearly identify which case study you are representing and provide a link to its ACM page.
Group Member Names and Roles. List and identify the names and role for each group member.

Spelling & Grammar (/2). Spelling, grammar, and punctuation.
2 to >1.0 pts: Full Marks - No spelling, grammar, or punctuation errors.
1 to >0.0 pts: Partial - Minimal spelling, grammar, or punctuation errors.
0 pts: No Marks - Consistent errors throughout.

Playability (/3). The scenario is complete and playable without additional instructions.
3 to >2.0 pts: Full Marks - Game is fully playable without additional instructions.
2 to > 0.0 pts: Partial - Unclear what to do or UI layout is inconsistent for gameplay.
0 pts: No Marks - Broken links, incomplete sections, or game is not playable as-is.

Originality (/2). The scenario extends or modifies the original case study text in some way, i.e., don't just copy the text from the source.
2 pts: Full Marks - The presented scenario is unique or substantially different from the source material, i.e., it's not a copy of the scenario.
1 pt: Partial - The story is a copy with only minor changes from the original case study.
0 pts: No Marks - The story is a copy of the ACM case study.

Realistic Choices (/3). The choices presented are realistic and coherent with the case study. For example, the character actions and choices are believable rather than hyperbolic or farcical.
3 to >2.0 pts: Full Marks - The character(s) is/are believable and their choices are consistent with the case and the characters.
2 to >0.0 pts: Partial - The choices do not appear to be realistic or are overly simple.
0 pts: No Marks - Choices are overly simplistic, are not really choices, or are not consistent with the character.

Multiple perspectives (/5). More than one perspective is represented, i.e., that the story is branching based on different ideas or choices.
5 to >3.0 pts: Full Marks - Story presents the character with multiple perspectives, for example, good vs. evil, stay quiet vs. speak out, etc. Each choice has consequences.
3 to >1.0 pts: Partial - A single perspective is presented or choices are limited or do not branch, i.e., only a single choice.
1 to >0 pts: No Marks - The story does not branch or the branches are single choices.

Consistent Implementation (/5). The scenario is consistent with both the central ethical choices as well as the case under study, i.e., the scenario captures the essence or ideas of the case under study.
5 to >3.0 pts: Full Marks - The story is consistent with the central moral or ethical dilemma and the case under study.
3 to >1.0 pts: Partial - The story presented does present a coherent story, but it does not follow the ACM case study presented.
1 to >0 pts: No Marks - The story presented is not consistent with the original case study and instead is a disconnected story or narrative.

Visual Appeal (/5). The UI is appealing and consistent and beyond the stock Twine UI.
5 to >3.0 pts: Full Marks - The UI is appealing, consistent, easy to navigate and goes beyond the stock experience.
3 to >1.0 pts: Partial - The UI is appealing and consistent, but does not go beyond the stock UI.
1 to >0 pts: No Marks - The UI is inconsistent (mixed fonts) or unappealing (poor color choices, misaligned items, etc.).
